Sherry FitzGerald McDermott are delighted to present Mons Lodge, a former officers residence dating back to c.1830. Steeped in character, this charming home has been extensively restored and thoughtfully brought into the modern era, while retaining many of its architectural features.

Set within a quiet tree lined cul de sac, Mons Lodge enjoys a wonderful balance of privacy and convienience, while being within walking distance of all town amenities.

Viewing by appointment is highly reccommended to fully appreciate the character and quality of this exceptional home.
